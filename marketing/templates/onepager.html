Why owners choose it

01 Zero tracking. Full stop.

No app, account, email, or phone number — ever. It never touches your POS and adds no work for your bartenders. One physical tap and they're rating.

02 Three punches, one deal

Bars run on regulars, so the reward comes fast: three taps = a deal you choose (say, a free well drink or $5 off a tab). The card lives on their phone — nothing to stamp or lose.

03 An opening offer that drives votes

Launch with a short, limited-time offer to pull new drinkers in and stack up verified ratings fast — while everyone's paying attention.

04 Ratings you can't game

Upvotes and stars only — one physical tap per phone a day. No downvotes, no comments, no review-bombing. Upvotes fade after 14 days, so your score shows who's hot right now, not last year.

05 You control the spotlight

A "Bartender's Choice" billboard for your top pours, plus a Happy Hour that flips on by your own schedule — all from one dashboard.
